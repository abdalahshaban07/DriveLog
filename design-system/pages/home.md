# Home

Macrostructure: identity plate (nickname, spec, tank, plate, odometer) with a since-last-fill rail (days, km, estimated range, station) → fuel pulse (economy spark, last fill, next due, month spend vs last month) → quick log → status → month outlook → what's next → period mix → documents. The since-last-fill rail shares the plate; it is not a second card.

Primary CTA: Log fill-up only. Progressive disclosure for vehicle/recalls. Public-data L/E/E/S required.

Around / nearby POI lives on the dedicated **Around** tab (`/around`), not on Home.
